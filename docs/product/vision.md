# Product vision

Status: evolving  
Last reviewed: 2026-07-19

## Identity

This is Sophon's personal digital space: a calm, atmospheric interface for exploring work, writing, visual practice, travel, and the ideas connecting them.

The site should feel personal, precise, editorial, and technically crafted. Its technical sophistication should support the content rather than become the content.

## Visitor experience

A visitor should be able to:

- understand who Sophon is and what Sophon cares about;
- explore selected projects and visual work;
- read essays, notes, and technical reflections;
- browse travel records and personal observations;
- move from the immersive homepage into clear, conventional content pages;
- use the site comfortably on a phone, with a keyboard, with reduced motion, or without WebGL.

## Experience hierarchy

### Homepage

Identity-first and visual-first. It may use terrain, motion, spatial navigation, and controlled atmosphere to create curiosity.

### Section pages

Content-first and visual-light. They organize projects, writing, art, and blog into browsable editorial surfaces.

### Detail pages

Reading-first and visual-minimal. Articles and case studies prioritize typography, media, metadata, and comprehension.

## Current priorities

1. Finish and consolidate the homepage identity.
2. Define the transition from the homepage into scrolling content.
3. Test the identity against one real content journey rather than designing every archive at once.
4. Establish a maintainable writing and portfolio foundation.
5. Add SEO, accessibility, mobile, and performance verification as built-in quality constraints.

## Anti-goals

The site should not become:

- a generic portfolio template;
- a fake operating system or noisy sci-fi dashboard;
- a Three.js demo whose content is inaccessible without the canvas;
- a mandatory cinematic sequence before visitors can navigate;
- a design system built from abstract tokens before representative pages exist;
- a complex publishing platform before real publishing needs emerge.
